## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene luxury, natural permanence, and refined architectural heritage of a flagship plotted residential development. Drawing from the traditions of high-end editorial property monographs and bespoke architectural publications, the aesthetic combines classical balance with contemporary restraint. 

The visual identity relies on generous spatial breathing room, deliberate asymmetrical balance, precise hairline metallic details, and organic green tones that anchor the experience to landscape and land stewardship. The emotional tone is reassuring, commanding, and timelessly sophisticated—evoking quiet wealth rather than loud opulence.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the deep, resonant weight of botanical evergreen and the luminous warmth of aged champagne brass, grounded upon stratified alabaster and warm paper neutrals.

### Roles & Applications
- **Primary (`#0F2B20`):** Forest evergreen. Serves as the primary typographic color, dominant brand anchors, high-authority status badges, and active state highlights. Ensures absolute legibility and an enduring, grounded authority.
- **Secondary (`#B8934A`):** Polished warm brass. Used for secondary CTA treatments, luxury accent badges, key metric counters, active navigation indicators, and decorative rule dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880`):** Warm champagne gold. Reserved for subtle borders, iconography embellishments, hover rings, and delicate structural hairlines.
- **Neutral Foundations:**
  - **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity primary presentation plane for imagery and interactive cards.
  - **Surface Level 1 (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm alabaster backdrop, delivering an editorial tactile paper presence without visual fatigue.
  - **Surface Level 2 (`#F4F1EA`):** Subdued architectural stone, utilized for architectural specs, section alternating blocks, and data card bays.
  - **Border & Hairlines (`#E5E1D8`):** Low-contrast structural rules that frame plots, architectural drawings, and card contours.
- **Text On Tone:**
  - Deep evergreen `#0F2B20` delivers high contrast against all alabaster and white surfaces.
  - Pure white `#FFFFFF` or pale cream `#FBF9F5` is reserved for text overlaying primary evergreen badges and hero modules.

## Typography

The typographical treatment brings together the classical elegance of high-contrast transitional serifs with the geometric clarity of modern sans-serif engineering.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for site titles, section intros, thematic narrative lines, and numerical metric highlights. Its delicate serifs and high contrast provide the tactile cadence of high-end print architectural folios.
- **Technical Body & Specifications (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures clear legibility across plot dimensions, zoning disclosures, financial estimates, and navigation labels.
- **Micro-Labels & Classifications:** Set in uppercase `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`) to produce quiet, museum-like labeling above plot tags, status chips, and architectural features.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a 12-column symmetrical grid on desktop and a 4-column structured flow on mobile, bounded within a maximum content envelope of 80rem (1280px).

### Structural Principles
- **Uncompressed White Space:** Section vertical margins scale deliberately (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`), honoring the quiet, unhurried posture of premier architectural brochures.
- **Strict Axial Alignment:** Card elements, metadata clusters, and gallery carousels align to strict vertical baselines. Information density is kept low-to-medium to encourage contemplation.
- **Form Factor Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 32px (`space-xl`) gutters, and generous page margins. Specification modules and master plans display as side-by-side balanced spreads.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px (`space-lg`) gutters. Master plan interactive overlays collapse into tabbed panels below media.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`space-md`) gutters, with edge-to-edge bleed options for high-resolution property renders framed by a subtle top and bottom hairline border.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface stratification, fine architectural hairline framing, and ambient, warm-tinted atmospheric drop shadows rather than heavy artificial elevation.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Underlying natural tone `#FBF9F5` or pure `#FFFFFF`. Completely flat, non-elevated.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting atop warm alabaster surfaces. Defined by a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E5E1D8`) combined with a diffused, sunlit shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 43, 32, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(184, 147, 74, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Master Plan Modals):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces lifted with a structural shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(15, 43, 32, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px #E5E1D8`.
- **Architectural Framing:** Modals and feature imagery leverage double-line hairline frames (an inner 1px border spaced 4px from an outer border) in champagne gold `#C5A880` at 30% opacity to evoke formal architectural blueprints.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural discipline by utilizing `Soft` corner radius scales (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Base Radius (`4px` / `0.25rem`):** Applied to form fields, interactive micro-chips, and secondary buttons. Maintains geometric precision without the harshness of razor-sharp vertices.
- **Medium Radius (`8px` / `0.5rem`):** Applied to architectural property cards, plot detail overlays, and map container modules.
- **Large Radius (`12px` / `0.75rem`):** Reserved strictly for high-level interactive modal windows and panoramic gallery frame viewports.
- **Pure Circular (`pill`):** Reserved exclusively for directional carousel chevrons and plot status indicator dots (e.g., Available, Reserved, Sold).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#0F2B20`, label `#FFFFFF` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600, letter spacing `0.04em`. Subtle 1px inner border in `#C5A880` at 20% opacity. On hover, background shifts softly to `#153E2E` with a subtle champagne glow (`0 0 16px rgba(184, 147, 74, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Luxury CTA:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #B8934A`, label `#0F2B20`. On hover, fills with warm brass `#B8934A` with white typography.
- **Text Action:** Playfair Display or Plus Jakarta Sans with an animated horizontal hairline underline in `#B8934A` that expands outward from the center upon hover.

### Cards (Plot & Residence Cards)
- Grounded on `#FFFFFF` with a precise `1px solid #E5E1D8` border.
- Features an integrated 16:10 ratio photograph slot, topped with an absolute positioned plot dimension badge in translucent `#0F2B20` (85% opacity) with gold lettering.
- Content body features high-contrast `Playfair Display` titling, an emerald-to-brass divider line, and a 3-column metric matrix (Area, Facing, Aspect) set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (e.g., 'Phase III Exclusive', 'Clubhouse Facing'):** Background `#F4F1EA`, border `1px solid #C5A880`, text `#0F2B20` styled in uppercase `label-caps`.
- **Availability Dots:** 6px circular nodes. Green `#0F2B20` for Available, Warm Gold `#B8934A` for Under Allotment, Soft Sandstone `#D1CCC0` for Sold.

### Form Inputs & Lead Inquiries
- Background `#FFFFFF` surrounded by `1px solid #E5E1D8`. On focus, border sharpens to `#0F2B20` with a 2px offset ring in `#B8934A` at 25% opacity.
- Floating labels transitions from `body-md` in muted earth tones to `label-caps` in `#0F2B20`.

### Plot Selector & Interactive Spec Sheets
- Tabular data presented with razor-thin horizontal rules (`#E5E1D8`), alternate row fills using `#FBF9F5`, and aligned right numerical specifications in tabular numerals.
- Interactive master plot SVG outlines hover-highlight in `#B8934A` with a soft 15% `#0F2B20` transparent fill.
